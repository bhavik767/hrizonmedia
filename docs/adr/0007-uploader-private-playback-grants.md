# Uploader-private Media Assets and Playback Grants

The Pilot Workspace is shared, but Media Assets remain private to their uploader; operators can inspect and manage assets without acquiring another uploader's playback permission. Playback requires an active owner, a ready and unexpired asset, and an asset-scoped five-minute Playback Grant before licence acquisition. Grant expiry limits playback starts rather than watch time, while deletion and retention expiry revoke playback immediately.
